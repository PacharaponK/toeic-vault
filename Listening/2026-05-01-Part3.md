## 📝 Questions

---

### Conversation 1 — Questions 1–3

**1.** What are the speakers discussing?

(A) A malfunctioning machine
(B) A software update
(C) A missing document
(D) A scheduling conflict

---

**2.** When was the technician scheduled to arrive?

(A) Earlier that morning
(B) The previous day
(C) The following week
(D) Two days ago

---

**3.** What will the woman probably do next?

(A) Contact the technician directly
(B) Send the files electronically
(C) Use a printer on another floor
(D) Postpone her meeting

---

### Conversation 2 — Questions 4–6

**4.** What is the main topic of the conversation?

(A) Updating financial software
(B) Hiring a new employee
(C) Planning a training session
(D) Reviewing a company budget

---

**5.** What concern does the man mention about the candidate?

(A) Her lack of references
(B) Her limited availability
(C) Her salary expectations
(D) Her unfamiliarity with certain software

---

**6.** Why does the woman say, "We can offer training"?

(A) To address the man's concern about the candidate
(B) To suggest postponing the hiring decision
(C) To explain why training is expensive
(D) To recommend a different candidate

---

### Conversation 3 — Questions 7–9

**7.** What are the speakers mainly discussing?

(A) Selecting a client gift
(B) Choosing a new venue
(C) Reducing event expenses
(D) Hiring additional staff

---

**8.** What does the woman suggest?

(A) Reducing the guest list
(B) Moving to a different location
(C) Ordering less expensive food
(D) Canceling the event entirely

---

**9.** What will the man probably do next?

(A) Book the conference center
(B) Contact the hotel manager
(C) Send invitations to clients
(D) Review the catering options

---

### Conversation 4 — Questions 10–12

**10.** Why are the speakers taking a train?

(A) To visit a factory
(B) To attend a client meeting
(C) To inspect a new office
(D) To meet a job candidate

---

**11.** Look at the graphic. Which train will the speakers most likely take?

(A) Express A
(B) Express B
(C) Regular A
(D) Regular B

---

**12.** What does the woman say she will do?

(A) Purchase the train tickets
(B) Print the presentation slides
(C) Contact the client
(D) Check the weather forecast
