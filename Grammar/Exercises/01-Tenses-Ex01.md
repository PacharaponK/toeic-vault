## โจทย์

**ข้อที่ 1**
The sales team _______ its weekly performance report every Friday morning before the department meeting.

- (A) is submitting
- (B) submitted
- (C) submits
- (D) has submitted

---

**ข้อที่ 2**
The company _______ its annual revenue target in the third quarter of last year.

- (A) exceeds
- (B) has exceeded
- (C) will exceed
- (D) exceeded

---

**ข้อที่ 3**
The board of directors _______ the merger proposal at its next quarterly meeting.

- (A) discussed
- (B) discusses
- (C) will discuss
- (D) has discussed

---

**ข้อที่ 4**
The engineering team _______ the upgraded payment system next Monday morning.

- (A) launches
- (B) launched
- (C) will have launched
- (D) is launching

---

**ข้อที่ 5**
The director _______ the annual budget report when the fire alarm suddenly went off.

- (A) reviewed
- (B) has reviewed
- (C) was reviewing
- (D) had reviewed

---

**ข้อที่ 6**
This time next week, our representatives _______ with potential investors at the Singapore summit.

- (A) will meet
- (B) have met
- (C) are meeting
- (D) will be meeting

---

**ข้อที่ 7**
The project manager _______ the final draft before the client arrived for the review meeting.

- (A) prepared
- (B) has prepared
- (C) had prepared
- (D) was preparing

---

**ข้อที่ 8**
By the end of this fiscal year, the company _______ its total outstanding debt by 30 percent.

- (A) reduces
- (B) will reduce

- (D) will have reduced

---

**ข้อที่ 9**
The research team _______ the new drug formula for the past six months with promising results.

- (A) develops
- (B) developed

- (D) had developed

---

**ข้อที่ 10**
The two executives _______ each other since they attended the same business school a decade ago.

- (A) are knowing
- (B) knew
- (C) have known
- (D) know

## เฉลยและคำอธิบาย

> [!warning] หยุดก่อน! ตอบทุกข้อแล้วค่อยเปิดดู

**ข้อ 1 → C (submits)**
"every Friday morning" = routine/habit → Simple Present (S + V(s/es))
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#simple-tenses]]

**ข้อ 2 → D (exceeded)**
"last year" = ระบุเวลาอดีตชัดเจน เหตุการณ์จบแล้ว → Simple Past
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#simple-tenses]]

**ข้อ 3 → C (will discuss)**
"next quarterly meeting" = เหตุการณ์อนาคต → Simple Future (will + V)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#simple-tenses]]

**ข้อ 4 → D (is launching)**
"next Monday" + แผนที่วางไว้แล้ว → Present Continuous แทน Future ได้ (already arranged)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#continuous--progressive-tenses]]

**ข้อ 5 → C (was reviewing)**
"went off" ขัดจังหวะกริยาที่กำลังดำเนินอยู่ → Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#continuous--progressive-tenses]]

**ข้อ 6 → D (will be meeting)**
"this time next week" = จุดเวลาอนาคตที่กริยาจะกำลังดำเนินอยู่ → Future Continuous (will be + V-ing)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#continuous--progressive-tenses]]

**ข้อ 7 → C (had prepared)**
"before the client arrived" = เหตุการณ์เกิดก่อนอีกเหตุการณ์ในอดีต → Past Perfect (had + V3)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#perfect-tenses]]

**ข้อ 8 → D (will have reduced)**
"by the end of this fiscal year" = by + เวลาอนาคต → Future Perfect (will have + V3)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#perfect-tenses]]

**ข้อ 9 → C (has been developing)**
"for the past six months" + ยังดำเนินอยู่ → Present Perfect Progressive (have/has + been + V-ing)
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#perfect-progressive-tenses]]

**ข้อ 10 → C (have known)**
"since" ต้องคู่กับ Present Perfect และ "know" เป็น state verb ห้ามใช้ progressive
→ [[Grammar/Rules/01-Tenses#กฎสำคัญสำหรับ-toeic]]
