## 📝 โจทย์ (Questions 1–10)

**Directions:** Choose the word or phrase that best completes each sentence.

---

**1.** The marketing department launched a _______ initiative to increase brand awareness across all platforms.

(A) creatively  
(B) creation  
(C) creative  
(D) creativity  

---

**2.** After reviewing all submitted proposals, the selection committee remained _______ about which vendor to choose.

(A) undecidedly  
(B) undeciding  
(C) undecided  
(D) undecision  

---

**3.** The head of department is _______ available to meet with staff members after the morning briefing.

(A) usual  
(B) usualness  
(C) more usual  
(D) usually  

---

**4.** Despite the tight deadline, the engineering team worked _______ to deliver the system upgrade on schedule.

(A) hardness  
(B) hardly  
(C) hard  
(D) harder  

---

**5.** The new regional director is _______ respected by colleagues throughout the Asia-Pacific division.

(A) high  
(B) higher  
(C) highly  
(D) heighten  

---

**6.** The project costs have increased _______ since the initial budget was approved six months ago.

(A) considerable  
(B) considerably  
(C) consideration  
(D) consider  

---

**7.** Participants found the two-day leadership workshop _______ and immediately applicable to their daily responsibilities.

(A) motivate  
(B) motivation  
(C) motivated  
(D) motivating  

---

**8.** New employees are _______ required to attend an orientation session during their first week.

(A) typical  
(B) typicality  
(C) typically  
(D) typicalness  

---

**9.** The company has invested in a _______ inventory tracking system to reduce warehouse errors.

(A) new reliable automated  
(B) automated new reliable  
(C) reliable new automated  
(D) reliable automated new  

---

**10.** The renovation project has become _______ complex, requiring additional consultation from external specialists.

(A) extreme  
(B) extremeness  
(C) extremely  
(D) extremity

## ✅ เฉลยและคำอธิบาย

---

### ข้อ 1 — คำตอบ: **(C) creative**

> The marketing department launched a **creative** initiative...

ช่องว่างอยู่หน้า noun "initiative" → ต้องการ **adjective** (attributive position)  
- (A) creatively = adverb ❌  
- (B) creation = noun ❌  
- (D) creativity = noun ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#adjective-position-rules]]

---

### ข้อ 2 — คำตอบ: **(C) undecided**

> ...the selection committee **remained** undecided...

**remained** = linking verb → ต้องตามด้วย **adjective** (predicative position)  
- (A) undecidedly = adverb ❌  
- (B) undeciding = present participle ไม่เหมาะกับ linking verb ❌  
- (D) undecision = noun ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#หลัง-linking-verb-predicative]]

---

### ข้อ 3 — คำตอบ: **(D) usually**

> The head of department **is usually** available...

โครงสร้าง: Subject + **be** + **[frequency adverb]** + adjective  
- (A) usual = adjective ไม่ขยาย adjective อีกตัวหนึ่งได้ ❌  
- (B)(C) noun และ comparative ผิด word form ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#ตำแหน่ง-frequency-adverbs]]

---

### ข้อ 4 — คำตอบ: **(C) hard**

> ...the engineering team worked **hard**...

**hard** (adverb) = "อย่างหนัก" → ขยาย verb "worked"  
**hardly** = "แทบจะไม่" → ความหมายตรงข้ามกับที่ประโยคต้องการ ❌  
- (A) hardness = noun ❌  
- (D) harder = comparative adjective ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#adjective-vs-adverb-ที่มักสับสน]]

---

### ข้อ 5 — คำตอบ: **(C) highly**

> ...is **highly** respected by colleagues...

**highly** (adverb) + past participle adjective "respected" = "ได้รับการยกย่องอย่างสูง"  
**high** (adverb) = สูงในเชิงกายภาพ เช่น "fly high" ❌  
- (B) higher = comparative ❌  
- (D) heighten = verb ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#adjective-vs-adverb-ที่มักสับสน]]

---

### ข้อ 6 — คำตอบ: **(B) considerably**

> The project costs have increased **considerably**...

ขยาย verb "increased" → ต้องการ **adverb of degree**  
- (A) considerable = adjective ❌  
- (C) consideration = noun ❌  
- (D) consider = verb ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#adverb-types]]

---

### ข้อ 7 — คำตอบ: **(D) motivating**

> Participants found the two-day leadership workshop **motivating**...

workshop (สิ่งของ/สิ่งที่ทำให้รู้สึก) → ใช้ **-ing** participial adjective  
**motivated** = ความรู้สึกของ*คน*ที่ถูกกระตุ้น ❌  
- (A) motivate = verb ❌  
- (B) motivation = noun ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#participial-adjectives]]

---

### ข้อ 8 — คำตอบ: **(C) typically**

> New employees are **typically** required...

ขยาย verb phrase "are required" → ต้องการ **adverb of frequency/manner**  
- (A) typical = adjective ❌  
- (B)(D) typicality / typicalness = noun ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#adverb-types]]

---

### ข้อ 9 — คำตอบ: **(C) reliable new automated**

> ...a **reliable new automated** inventory tracking system...

ลำดับ adjective: **Opinion → Age → Material/Purpose**  
- reliable = Opinion (ความเห็น)  
- new = Age (อายุ)  
- automated = Purpose/Material (ลักษณะเฉพาะ)  

(A) "new reliable automated" → Age ก่อน Opinion ❌  
(B)(D) ลำดับผิดทั้งคู่ ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#ลำดับ-adjective-หลายคำ-order-of-adjectives]]

---

### ข้อ 10 — คำตอบ: **(C) extremely**

> ...has become **extremely** complex...

**become** = linking verb → ตามด้วย adjective "complex"  
ก่อน adjective "complex" ต้องการ **intensifier (adverb of degree)**  
- (A) extreme = adjective ไม่ขยาย adjective อีกตัวได้ ❌  
- (B)(D) extremeness / extremity = noun ❌  

→ [[Grammar/Rules/09-Adjectives-Adverbs#intensifiers-ระดับความเข้ม]]
