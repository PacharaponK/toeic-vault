# 03 — Passive Voice · Exercise 1

[[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice]]

## โจทย์ (TOEIC Part 5 Style)

เลือกคำตอบที่ถูกต้องที่สุดสำหรับแต่ละประโยค

---

**1.** The annual report _______ by the finance team last quarter, and all figures were verified by an external auditor.

(A) prepares
(B) is prepared
(C) was prepared
(D) has been prepared

---

**2.** The safety guidelines _______ three times since the company was founded in 1998.

(A) revised
(B) have been revised
(C) are revised
(D) were being revised

---

**3.** The machinery _______ when the technician noticed a critical fault in the cooling system.

(A) was maintained
(B) is being maintained
(C) was being maintained
(D) had been maintained

---

**4.** Due to increased registration, the annual conference _______ in a larger venue next year.

(A) holds
(B) will hold
(C) will be held
(D) is being held

---

**5.** All visitor badges must _______ at the security desk before entering any restricted area of the facility.

(A) collect
(B) be collected
(C) be collecting
(D) have collected

---

**6.** Customer feedback _______ by the product team before any design changes are finalized.

(A) should consider
(B) should be considering
(C) should be considered
(D) should have consider

---

**7.** The new regional distribution center _______ just outside the city limits, near the main expressway.

(A) locates
(B) is locating
(C) is located
(D) has located

---

**8.** The project manager _______ coordinating communication between all departments during the office renovation.

(A) is responsible to
(B) responsible for
(C) is responsibly for
(D) is responsible for

---

**9.** The maintenance schedule _______ by the operations manager before it can be implemented across all facilities.

(A) needs to approve
(B) needs approving
(C) needs to be approved
(D) need be approved

---

**10.** By the end of this month, the new software system _______ by all members of the IT department.

(A) will be tested
(B) will have been tested
(C) has been tested
(D) is being tested

## เฉลยและคำอธิบาย

> **อย่าเปิดดูก่อนตอบครบทุกข้อ!**

---

**ข้อ 1 — คำตอบ: (C) was prepared** ✅

*last quarter* บอกเวลาในอดีต → ใช้ Simple Past Passive = **was/were + V3**
Subject "The annual report" เป็น singular → was prepared
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#โครงสร้าง-passive-ทุก-tense]]

---

**ข้อ 2 — คำตอบ: (B) have been revised** ✅

*since 1998* เป็น signal ของ Present Perfect → Passive = **have/has + been + V3**
Subject "The safety guidelines" เป็น plural → have been revised
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#โครงสร้าง-passive-ทุก-tense]]

---

**ข้อ 3 — คำตอบ: (C) was being maintained** ✅

เหตุการณ์ต่อเนื่องในอดีตที่ถูกขัดจังหวะ → Past Continuous Passive = **was/were + being + V3**
ใช้ was (singular) → was being maintained
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#โครงสร้าง-passive-ทุก-tense]]

---

**ข้อ 4 — คำตอบ: (C) will be held** ✅

*next year* = อนาคต → Simple Future Passive = **will + be + V3**
(A) holds = active present, (B) will hold = active future → ไม่ใช่ passive
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#โครงสร้าง-passive-ทุก-tense]]

---

**ข้อ 5 — คำตอบ: (B) be collected** ✅

*must* เป็น modal verb → Passive = **must + be + V3**
(A) collect = active, (C) be collecting = ไม่ถูก form, (D) have collected = active perfect
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#passive--modal-verbs]]

---

**ข้อ 6 — คำตอบ: (C) should be considered** ✅

*should* เป็น modal verb → Passive = **should + be + V3**
(A) should consider = active, (D) should have consider = ผิด grammar
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#passive--modal-verbs]]

---

**ข้อ 7 — คำตอบ: (C) is located** ✅

*be located in/near* เป็น Stative Passive แสดงสถานะ (ตั้งอยู่ที่)
(A) locates = active ไม่เหมาะ, (B) is locating = ไม่ใช้ continuous กับ stative
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#stative-passive-ใช้บ่อยใน-toeic]]

---

**ข้อ 8 — คำตอบ: (D) is responsible for** ✅

*be responsible for* เป็น Stative Passive แสดงความรับผิดชอบ
(A) is responsible to = ผิด preposition, (B) ขาด verb, (C) ผิด adverb
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#stative-passive-ใช้บ่อยใน-toeic]]

---

**ข้อ 9 — คำตอบ: (C) needs to be approved** ✅

*need to be + V3* = Passive Infinitive → ถูกต้อง
(A) needs to approve = active ความหมายผิด (schedule ไม่ได้ approve เอง)
(D) need be approved = ผิด structure
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#toeic-part-5-passive-patterns-ที่ออกบ่อย]]

---

**ข้อ 10 — คำตอบ: (B) will have been tested** ✅

*By the end of this month* + อนาคต → Future Perfect Passive = **will + have + been + V3**
(A) will be tested = Future Passive ธรรมดา ไม่เน้นว่าจะเสร็จก่อนจุดอ้างอิง
→ [[Grammar/Rules/03-Passive-Voice#โครงสร้าง-passive-ทุก-tense]]
