# 09 — Adjectives & Adverbs

## Adjective Position Rules

### ก่อน Noun (Attributive)
> a **successful** launch / an **efficient** system / **comprehensive** training

### หลัง Linking Verb (Predicative)
> The results are **promising**.
> The proposal seems **feasible**.
> The manager became **available** after lunch.

### Linking Verbs ที่ตามด้วย Adjective
appear, be, become, feel, get, grow, look, remain, seem, smell, sound, stay, taste, turn

> The situation **remains** critical. ✓ (ไม่ใช่ critically)
> The new policy **seems** effective. ✓ (ไม่ใช่ effectively)

---

## Adverb Types

| ประเภท        | คำถาม      | ตัวอย่าง                                          |
| ------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Frequency** | How often? | always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never  |
| **Manner**    | How?       | quickly, efficiently, carefully, well, hard       |
| **Degree**    | How much?  | very, quite, rather, fairly, extremely, slightly  |
| **Time**      | When?      | now, soon, already, recently, yesterday, annually |
| **Place**     | Where?     | here, there, abroad, nearby, worldwide            |

### ตำแหน่ง Frequency Adverbs
> Subject + **[frequency adverb]** + main verb
> She **always** submits reports on time.
> They **rarely** cancel meetings.
> Subject + be + **[frequency adverb]**
> He is **usually** available in the morning.

## Adjective vs Adverb ที่มักสับสน

| Adjective | Adverb | ความแตกต่าง |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **hard** | **hard** | He works **hard**. (hard ใช้เป็นทั้ง adj และ adv) |
| — | **hardly** | She **hardly** ever complains. (= แทบจะไม่) |
| **late** | **late** | He arrived **late**. (= สาย) |
| — | **lately** | **Lately**, sales have improved. (= เร็วๆ นี้) |
| **near** | **near** | The deadline is **near**. |
| — | **nearly** | We **nearly** missed the deadline. (= เกือบ) |
| **high** | **high** | The plane flew **high**. (= สูง) |
| — | **highly** | She is **highly** qualified. (= อย่างมาก) |
| **close** | **close** | Stand **close** to the screen. |
| — | **closely** | Monitor the situation **closely**. (= อย่างใกล้ชิด) |
| **deep** | **deep** | Dig **deep** into the data. |
| — | **deeply** | We are **deeply** concerned. (= อย่างมาก) |

## Participial Adjectives

### -ING (น่า...) vs -ED (รู้สึก...)
> The presentation was **boring**. → การนำเสนอน่าเบื่อ
> The audience was **bored**. → ผู้ฟังรู้สึกเบื่อ

| -ING (สิ่งที่ทำให้รู้สึก) | -ED (ความรู้สึกของผู้ประสบ) |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| interesting | interested |
| confusing | confused |
| satisfying | satisfied |
| challenging | challenged |
| encouraging | encouraged |
| convincing | convinced |
| disappointing | disappointed |
| overwhelming | overwhelmed |
| exhausting | exhausted |
| motivating | motivated |

## ลำดับ Adjective หลายคำ (Order of Adjectives)

> เมื่อใช้ adjective หลายคำขยาย noun เดียว ต้องเรียงตามลำดับนี้:

**Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Color → Origin → Material → Purpose + NOUN**

| ลำดับ | หมวด | ตัวอย่าง |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Opinion (ความเห็น) | excellent, useful, innovative, reliable |
| 2 | Size (ขนาด) | large, small, compact, extensive |
| 3 | Age (อายุ) | new, old, recent, established |
| 4 | Shape (รูปร่าง) | round, square, flat, narrow |
| 5 | Color (สี) | blue, black, white, silver |
| 6 | Origin (ที่มา) | American, Japanese, domestic, foreign |
| 7 | Material (วัสดุ) | glass, metal, digital, paper |
| 8 | Purpose (จุดประสงค์) | filing (cabinet), meeting (room), training (manual) |

**ตัวอย่างในบริบท TOEIC Business:**
> a **large** **modern** conference room
> an **excellent** **new** **Japanese** accounting software
> a **reliable** **compact** **digital** filing system
> the **annual** **financial** report

> **กฎง่ายๆ**: opinion → fact (ทั่วไป → เฉพาะเจาะจง)
> ใน TOEIC มักมีแค่ 2-3 adjective → ตรวจว่า opinion มาก่อน fact

## Intensifiers — ระดับความเข้ม

| ระดับ | คำ | ตัวอย่าง |
|-------|-----|---------|
| สูงมาก | extremely, exceptionally, remarkably | **Extremely** competitive |
| สูง | very, highly, quite (British) | **Highly** recommended |
| ปานกลาง | fairly, rather, somewhat, reasonably | **Fairly** straightforward |
| น้อย | slightly, a little, a bit | **Slightly** above budget |

> ใช้กับ ungradable adjectives → **absolutely** perfect / **completely** wrong (ไม่ใช่ very)

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| The team performed **good**. | The team performed **well**. | perform = action verb → adverb |
| She is a **highly** talented manager. | ✓ | highly + adjective = ถูกต้อง |
| He worked **hardly** on the project. | He worked **hard** on the project. | hardly = แทบจะไม่ (ไม่ใช่ "อย่างหนัก") |
| The results are **differently** from expected. | The results are **different** from expected. | หลัง be → adjective |
| She sounded **angrily** on the phone. | She sounded **angry** on the phone. | sounded = linking verb → adjective |
| The report is **well-written** quality. | The report is of **well-written** quality. | ตรวจโครงสร้างด้วย |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (6 ข้อ)

1. The customer service team responded _______ to all complaints within 24 hours.
   **(A) prompt (B) promptly (C) promptness (D) prompted**
   → **B** (ขยาย verb "responded" → adverb)

2. The new accounting software proved _______ for the entire finance department.
   **(A) use (B) useful (C) usefully (D) usability**
   → **B** (proved = linking verb → adjective)

3. Sales figures for the third quarter were _______ higher than projected.
   **(A) slight (B) slighting (C) slightly (D) slighted**
   → **C** (ขยาย adjective "higher" → adverb)

4. The training program received _______ reviews from all participants.
   **(A) excite (B) excited (C) exciting (D) excitement**
   → **C** (program เป็นสิ่งที่ทำให้รู้สึก → -ing)

5. All employees are _______ encouraged to participate in the wellness program.
   **(A) strong (B) strongly (C) strength (D) stronger**
   → **B** (ขยาย verb "encouraged" → adverb)

6. Ms. Park remained _______ throughout the difficult negotiation.
   **(A) calm (B) calmly (C) calming (D) calmed**
   → **A** (remained = linking verb → adjective)

7. The company has developed a _______ automated inventory management system.
   **(A) reliable new compact (B) new reliable compact (C) compact reliable new (D) reliable compact new**
   → **A** (opinion → size/quality → age: reliable = opinion, compact = size, new = age)

8. The auditors were _______ impressed by the thoroughness of the financial records.
   **(A) high (B) highly (C) higher (D) highest**
   → **B** (ขยาย adjective "impressed" → adverb; highly + past participle adj = ถูกต้อง)
