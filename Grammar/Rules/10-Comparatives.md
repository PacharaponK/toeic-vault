# 10 — Comparatives & Superlatives

## Regular Forms

### Comparative (-er / more + than)
| พยางค์ | กฎ | ตัวอย่าง |
|--------|-----|---------|
| 1 พยางค์ | + -er | fast → **faster**, cheap → **cheaper** |
| 1 พยางค์ ending CVC | double final consonant + -er | big → **bigger**, hot → **hotter** |
| 2 พยางค์ ending -y | change y → -ier | easy → **easier**, busy → **busier** |
| 2+ พยางค์ | more + adj | efficient → **more efficient** |

### Superlative (-est / most)
| พยางค์ | กฎ | ตัวอย่าง |
|--------|-----|---------|
| 1 พยางค์ | the + -est | fast → **the fastest** |
| 2+ พยางค์ | the most + adj | efficient → **the most efficient** |

> ใช้ **the** เสมอกับ superlative

---

## Irregular Forms

| Adjective/Adverb | Comparative | Superlative |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| good / well | better | best |
| bad / badly | worse | worst |
| far | farther / further | farthest / furthest |
| little (amount) | less | least |
| many / much | more | most |
| late | later (เวลา) / latter (ลำดับ) | latest (ล่าสุด) / last (สุดท้าย) |
| old | older (อายุ) / elder (ลำดับพี่น้อง) | oldest / eldest |

---

## As...As Construction

| โครงสร้าง | ความหมาย | ตัวอย่าง |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| as + adj/adv + as | เท่ากัน | This model is **as efficient as** the previous one. |
| not as + adj/adv + as | น้อยกว่า | This plan is **not as costly as** the original. |
| twice / three times as...as | เท่า | Revenue is **twice as high as** last year. |
| as many/much + noun + as | จำนวนเท่ากัน | We hired **as many** staff **as** possible. |

---

## Double Comparative — The More...The More

> **The + comparative, the + comparative**
> **The more** you practice, **the better** you perform.
> **The higher** the quality, **the greater** the customer satisfaction.
> **The sooner** we start, **the faster** we finish.

---

## Comparative Structures ใน Business English

| Structure | ตัวอย่าง |
|-----------|---------|
| superior to | This product is **superior to** competitors'. |
| inferior to | The old model is **inferior to** the new one. |
| prefer A to B | We **prefer** digital communication **to** paper memos. |
| rather than | Use email **rather than** phone for records. |
| no less...than | This is **no less** important **than** profitability. |
| no more...than | The new process is **no more** expensive **than** the old. |

---

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| more better | **better** | better ไม่ต้องมี more |
| the most highest | **the highest** | ไม่ใช้ the most กับ superlative -est |
| more superior | **superior** | superior เป็น comparative อยู่แล้ว |
| more cheaper | **cheaper** | cheap เป็น 1 พยางค์ → -er เท่านั้น |
| as quick as possible | **as quickly as possible** | ขยาย verb → adverb |
| superior than | **superior to** | superior/inferior/prior → to |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (6 ข้อ)

1. The new manufacturing process is _______ efficient than the previous one.
   **(A) more (B) most (C) much (D) very**
   → **A** (comparative = more + adj + than)

2. Of all candidates interviewed, Ms. Lee presented _______ proposal.
   **(A) an impressive (B) more impressive (C) the most impressive (D) most impressive**
   → **C** (superlative after "of all" → the most)

3. This year's budget is nearly twice _______ last year's.
   **(A) as large as (B) larger (C) largest (D) as large)**
   → **A** (twice as + adj + as)

4. _______ the demand increases, the more we need to expand production.
   **(A) More (B) The more (C) The most (D) As more)**
   → **B** (double comparative = the more...the more)

5. Our service is _______ to any competitor in terms of reliability.
   **(A) better (B) superior (C) more superior (D) best)**
   → **B** (superior to — no "more" needed)

6. The revised proposal is _______ cost-effective than the original draft.
   **(A) far (B) farther (C) the far (D) far more)**
   → **D** (far more + comparative = เน้น "มากกว่ามาก")
