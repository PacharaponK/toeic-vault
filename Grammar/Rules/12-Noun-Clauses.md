# 12 — Noun Clauses

## That-Clauses หลัง Verb

> S + V + **that** + S + V

| ประเภท Verb | ตัวอย่าง Verb | ตัวอย่าง |
|------------|--------------|---------|
| **Thinking** | know, think, believe, assume, realize | I **know that** the report is ready. |
| **Saying** | say, tell, announce, state, claim | He **announced that** the merger is complete. |
| **Feeling** | feel, hope, wish, fear, expect | She **hopes that** the proposal is accepted. |
| **Suggesting** | suggest, recommend, request, demand | They **suggest that** we review the budget. |

### กฎ: "that" สามารถละได้ในการเขียนไม่เป็นทางการ
> We believe **(that)** the plan will succeed.
> แต่ใน TOEIC มักมี **that** เพื่อความชัดเจน

---

## WH-Word Clauses

> S + V + **wh-word** + S + V (word order = ประโยคบอกเล่า ไม่ใช่คำถาม)

| WH-Word | ตัวอย่าง |
|---------|---------|
| **what** | Please clarify **what the policy requires**. |
| **where** | We need to determine **where the meeting will be held**. |
| **when** | I don't know **when the results will be announced**. |
| **how** | The manual explains **how the system works**. |
| **why** | Can you explain **why the shipment was delayed**? |
| **who** | We haven't decided **who will lead the project**. |
| **which** | Please indicate **which option you prefer**. |
| **how much/many** | I'll check **how many units are in stock**. |

---

## If / Whether Clauses

> ใช้เมื่อ noun clause แสดงความไม่แน่ใจ (yes/no)

| กฎ | ตัวอย่าง |
|----|---------|
| if / whether ใช้แทนกันได้ | I wonder **if/whether** the deadline will change. |
| หลัง preposition → **whether** เท่านั้น | The decision depends on **whether** funding is approved. |
| ก่อน or not → **whether** | We must decide **whether or not** to proceed. / We must decide **whether** to proceed **or not**. |
| เป็น subject → **whether** เท่านั้น | **Whether** we succeed depends on teamwork. |

---

## Noun Clause เป็น Subject

> **That** + clause + verb (formal, มักใช้ it...that แทน)

| แบบปกติ | แบบ Formal (ที่ใช้ใน TOEIC) |
|---------|--------------------------|
| That the report is late is a concern. | **It is a concern that** the report is late. |
| What he said surprised everyone. | **It surprised everyone what** he said. |
| Whether we proceed depends on funding. | **It depends on funding whether** we proceed. |

---

## Subjunctive ใน Noun Clauses

> หลัง verbs ที่ suggest/recommend/request/require/demand/insist/propose
> โครงสร้าง: V + **that** + S + **V (base form)** — ไม่มี s/es, ไม่มี was/were

| ผิด | ถูก |
|-----|-----|
| The manager recommended that she **works** harder. | The manager recommended that she **work** harder. |
| They insisted that he **was** present. | They insisted that he **be** present. |
| We require that all staff **submits** reports. | We require that all staff **submit** reports. |

### Verbs ที่ trigger subjunctive
**recommend, suggest, propose, request, require, demand, insist, ask, urge, advise, mandate, stipulate**

---

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| I don't know **what does** the policy say. | I don't know **what the policy says**. | noun clause = declarative word order |
| Please explain **why did** he resign. | Please explain **why he resigned**. | wh-word + S + V (ไม่ใช่คำถาม) |
| It depends on **if** funding is approved. | It depends on **whether** funding is approved. | หลัง preposition → whether |
| She suggested that he **should review** it. | She suggested that he **review** it. | subjunctive = base form (ไม่มี should) |
| We asked **that did** he agree. | We asked **whether** he agreed. | reported yes/no question = whether |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (6 ข้อ)

1. The report clearly explains _______ the new system operates.
   **(A) that (B) what (C) how (D) which**
   → **C** (how the system operates = วิธีการ)

2. The committee recommends _______ all employees complete the safety training.
   **(A) that (B) what (C) which (D) whether**
   → **A** (recommend + that + subjunctive)

3. It remains unclear _______ the proposed changes will be implemented.
   **(A) that (B) what (C) when (D) which**
   → **C** (unclear when = ไม่ชัดว่าเมื่อไร)

4. The management insists that each department _______ a detailed budget proposal.
   **(A) submits (B) submit (C) will submit (D) submitted**
   → **B** (insists that + subjunctive = base form)

5. We need to confirm _______ the venue is available for next Thursday.
   **(A) that (B) whether (C) what (D) how**
   → **B** (yes/no uncertainty = whether/if)

6. _______ the two candidates are equally qualified makes the decision difficult.
   **(A) Whether (B) That (C) Because (D) What**
   → **A** (whether...makes = subject clause with uncertainty)
