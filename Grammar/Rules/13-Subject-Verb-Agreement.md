# 13 — Subject-Verb Agreement

## S-V Agreement พื้นฐาน

| Subject | Verb Form | ตัวอย่าง |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Singular | V + s/es | The manager **reviews** all reports. |
| Plural | V (base) | The managers **review** all reports. |
| Uncountable | Singular | Information **is** available online. |
| Gerund / Infinitive subject | Singular | **Attending** meetings **is** mandatory. |

---

## Collective Nouns — Singular ใน American English

> ใน TOEIC (American English) collective nouns ใช้ **singular verb**

| Collective Noun | ตัวอย่าง |
|----------------|---------|
| staff | The staff **was** briefed this morning. |
| team | Our team **has** completed the project. |
| committee | The committee **meets** every Monday. |
| board | The board **has** approved the merger. |
| management | Management **is** reviewing the proposal. |
| audience | The audience **was** impressed. |
| company | The company **plans** to expand. |

---

## Compound Subjects

### And → Plural
> The CEO **and** CFO **have** signed the agreement.
> Accuracy **and** efficiency **are** both required.

### Exception: ถ้าหมายถึงสิ่งเดียว → Singular
> Bread **and** butter **is** a staple food.
> The founder **and** CEO **is** retiring. (คนเดียวกัน)

### Or / Nor → ตาม Noun ที่ใกล้ที่สุด (Proximity Rule)
> Either the manager or the assistants **are** responsible.
> Neither the reports nor the summary **was** submitted.
> Either the assistants or the manager **is** responsible.

---

## Each / Every / Either / Neither → Singular เสมอ

| คำ | กฎ | ตัวอย่าง |
|----|-----|---------|
| **each** | + singular noun + singular verb | **Each** employee **has** a desk. |
| **every** | + singular noun + singular verb | **Every** report **needs** approval. |
| **either** | + singular noun + singular verb | **Either** option **is** acceptable. |
| **neither** | + singular noun + singular verb | **Neither** candidate **was** selected. |
| **each of** | + plural noun + singular verb | **Each of** the reports **is** ready. |

---

## Intervening Phrases — อย่าให้สับสน

> Subject + **[intervening phrase]** + Verb
> Verb ต้องตรงกับ **Subject ที่แท้จริง** ไม่ใช่ noun ในวลีคั่นกลาง

| Intervening Phrase | ตัวอย่าง |
|-------------------|---------|
| along with | The manager, **along with** his team, **is** attending. |
| as well as | The report, **as well as** the attachments, **was** sent. |
| together with | The CEO, **together with** the board, **has** approved. |
| in addition to | The price, **in addition to** the fees, **is** reasonable. |
| including | The package, **including** all accessories, **costs** $500. |
| with | The president, **with** all advisors, **attends** weekly. |

---

## There Is / There Are

> There + **be** verb + Subject (verb ตาม noun ที่ตามมา)

> **There is** a problem with the report.
> **There are** several issues to address.
> **There was** an announcement yesterday.
> **There have been** many changes this year.

---

## Special Cases

| กรณี | กฎ | ตัวอย่าง |
|------|-----|---------|
| None of | singular หรือ plural ได้ | **None of** the information **is** verified. |
| The number of | singular | **The number of** employees **has** increased. |
| A number of | plural | **A number of** employees **have** resigned. |
| All of the | ตาม noun | **All of the** data **is** ready. / **All of the** files **are** ready. |
| Percentage/Fraction + of | ตาม noun | 30% **of** the budget **is** used. / 30% **of** the employees **are** satisfied. |

## กรณีพิเศษเพิ่มเติม

### ชื่อประเทศ / บริษัท / หนังสือ / วิชา → Singular เสมอ
| กรณี | ตัวอย่าง |
|------|---------|
| ชื่อบริษัท | **Yamamoto & Associates is** expanding globally. |
| ชื่อหนังสือ/เอกสาร | **"The New Regulations" was** released last month. |
| วิชาเรียน | **Economics is** a required course. |
| ประเทศที่มี plural form | **The United States has** signed the treaty. |
| ระยะเวลา / จำนวนเงิน / ระยะทาง (รวมเป็นหน่วยเดียว) | **Three years is** a long time to wait. / **Five thousand dollars is** the budget. |

### Every / Each ก่อน Compound Subject → Singular
> **Every** manager and supervisor **is** required to attend.
> **Each** applicant and referee **has** submitted the form.

### Relative Clause ที่ขั้นระหว่าง
> The number of employees **who** work remotely **has** increased. (subject = the number)
> The staff members **who** were promoted **have** worked here for over 5 years. (subject = staff members)

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| The staff **are** working overtime. | The staff **is** working overtime. | collective noun → singular (AE) |
| Each of the reports **are** complete. | Each of the reports **is** complete. | each of + plural noun → singular verb |
| The number of complaints **have** increased. | The number of complaints **has** increased. | the number of → singular |
| A number of issues **has** arisen. | A number of issues **have** arisen. | a number of → plural |
| Neither the CEO nor directors **was** told. | Neither the CEO nor directors **were** told. | proximity → directors (plural) → were |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (8 ข้อ)

1. The committee _______ scheduled to meet on the first Monday of each month.
   **(A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have been**
   → **B** (committee = collective noun → singular)

2. Each of the quarterly reports _______ been reviewed by the audit team.
   **(A) have (B) has (C) were (D) are**
   → **B** (each of + plural noun → singular)

3. Neither the proposal nor the supporting documents _______ submitted on time.
   **(A) was (B) were (C) is (D) has been**
   → **B** (nor + documents (plural) → proximity → were)

4. The marketing team, along with the design department, _______ working on the campaign.
   **(A) are (B) were (C) is (D) have been**
   → **C** (subject = marketing team → singular; along with ไม่เปลี่ยน verb)

5. A number of employees _______ requested flexible working hours.
   **(A) has (B) have (C) is (D) was**
   → **B** (a number of → plural)

6. There _______ several discrepancies in the financial statement.
   **(A) is (B) has (C) are (D) was**
   → **C** (there + plural noun "discrepancies" → are)

7. Every manager and supervisor _______ required to attend the annual training.
   **(A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have been**
   → **B** (every + [noun] and [noun] → singular)

8. Forty percent of the workforce _______ eligible for the bonus.
   **(A) is (B) are (C) was (D) A or B depending on context**
   → **D** (workforce = collective/uncountable → is ใน AE)
