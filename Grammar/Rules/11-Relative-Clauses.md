# 11 — Relative Clauses

## Defining vs Non-Defining

| ประเภท | กฎ | ตัวอย่าง |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **Defining** (จำเป็นต่อความหมาย) | ไม่มี comma | The employee **who submitted late** was penalized. |
| **Non-defining** (ข้อมูลเสริม) | มี comma ล้อมรอบ | Mr. Park, **who joined last year**, was promoted. |

> ใน Defining clause → ใช้ **that** หรือ **which/who** ได้
> ใน Non-defining clause → ใช้ **which/who** เท่านั้น (ห้ามใช้ that)

---

## การเลือก Relative Pronoun

| Pronoun | ใช้กับ | บทบาท | ตัวอย่าง |
|---------|--------|-------|---------|
| **who** | คน | subject | The manager **who** approved the budget... |
| **whom** | คน | object | The candidate **whom** we selected... |
| **which** | สิ่ง / สัตว์ | subject หรือ object | The report **which** was submitted late... |
| **that** | คน / สิ่ง | subject หรือ object (defining only) | The document **that** you need... |
| **whose** | คน / สิ่ง | possession | The firm **whose** CEO resigned... |
| **where** | สถานที่ | adverbial | The office **where** we work... |
| **when** | เวลา | adverbial | The day **when** we launched... |

---

## Who vs Whom — เคล็ดลับ

แทนด้วย he/him ใน relative clause:
- ถ้าแทนได้ด้วย **he** → ใช้ **who**
- ถ้าแทนได้ด้วย **him** → ใช้ **whom**

> The manager **who** signed the contract. → **He** signed = who ✓
> The manager **whom** we hired. → We hired **him** = whom ✓
> To **whom** should I address this email? → formal, หลัง preposition

---

## Which vs That — กฎเลือก

| ใช้ **that** | ใช้ **which** |
|-------------|--------------|
| Defining clause (ทั่วไป) | Non-defining clause (มี comma) |
| หลัง superlative | หลัง preposition |
| หลัง the only / the same / all / nothing | เมื่อต้องการแยก clause ยาว |

> This is the **best** report **that** I have read.
> The proposal, **which** was submitted on Friday, was accepted.
> The system **in which** we invested proved effective.

---

## Whose — สำหรับ Possession

> The employee **whose** application impressed us was hired.
> The company **whose** profits declined last year has restructured.
> We need a supplier **whose** delivery times are reliable.

---

## Reduced Relative Clauses — ตัดให้สั้นลง

| เต็ม | ย่อ | กฎ |
|------|-----|-----|
| The report **which was** submitted last week | The report **submitted** last week | who/which + be → ตัดทั้งคู่ |
| The manager **who is** responsible for sales | The manager **responsible** for sales | who + be + adj → ตัดทั้งคู่ |
| The system **that is** used by all departments | The system **used** by all departments | passive participle |
| The team **that is** working on the project | The team **working** on the project | active participle |
| Employees **who work** overtime | Employees **working** overtime | relative subject + verb → present participle |

---

## What ≠ Relative Pronoun — ข้อผิดพลาดที่พบบ่อย

> **what** ทำหน้าที่เป็น noun clause (= "the thing that") **ไม่ใช่** relative pronoun ที่ขยาย noun

| ใช้ that/which/who | ใช้ what |
|--------------------|---------|
| The report **that** I submitted... (มี antecedent = report) | **What** I submitted was the final report. (ไม่มี antecedent) |
| The issue **that** concerns us... | **What** concerns us is the timeline. |
| The recommendation **which** was approved... | **What** was approved is the budget. |

> ❌ The thing **what** I need is approval. → ✓ The thing **that** I need is approval. / **What** I need is approval.

---

## Quantified Relative Clauses

> ใช้ของ + relative pronoun เพื่อแสดงปริมาณ

| โครงสร้าง | ตัวอย่าง |
|----------|---------|
| **most of which / many of which** | The reports, **most of which** were submitted late, needed revision. |
| **some of whom / all of whom** | The candidates, **all of whom** were qualified, were interviewed. |
| **none of which** | The proposals, **none of which** met the criteria, were rejected. |

---

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| The report, **that** was late, was rejected. | The report, **which** was late, was rejected. | Non-defining → ใช้ which ไม่ใช่ that |
| The manager **which** handles accounts | The manager **who** handles accounts | คน → who/whom (ไม่ใช่ which) |
| The office **where** I work **in** | The office **where** I work / **in which** I work | ไม่ต้องมี in หลัง where |
| The employee **whom** called | The employee **who** called | called = กริยา subject → who |
| The company **who** was founded | The company **which/that** was founded | บริษัท = สิ่ง → which/that |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (6 ข้อ)

1. The consultant _______ the company hired last month has already improved productivity.
   **(A) who (B) whom (C) which (D) whose**
   → **B** (the company hired him → him = whom)

2. The conference room, _______ accommodates up to 50 people, is available on Monday.
   **(A) that (B) which (C) who (D) what**
   → **B** (non-defining clause = comma → which)

3. The software _______ interface is the most intuitive will win the contract.
   **(A) who (B) which (C) whose (D) that**
   → **C** (software's interface = possession → whose)

4. Employees _______ for the overseas assignment must submit applications by Friday.
   **(A) applying (B) applied (C) who applying (D) are applying**
   → **A** (reduced relative clause: who are applying → applying)

5. The branch _______ our regional headquarters is located handles over 200 clients.
   **(A) that (B) where (C) which (D) in that**
   → **B** (สถานที่ = where)

6. The proposal _______ was presented at last week's meeting received unanimous approval.
   **(A) who (B) whose (C) that (D) whom**
   → **C** (defining clause, สิ่ง → that/which)

7. The quarterly reports, _______ were submitted late, caused a delay in the audit.
   **(A) most of them (B) most of which (C) most of those (D) most of what**
   → **B** (quantified relative clause: most of which)

8. _______ the management team found most surprising was the rapid decline in customer retention.
   **(A) That (B) Which (C) What (D) The thing that**
   → **C** (what = the thing that; ไม่มี antecedent → noun clause subject)
