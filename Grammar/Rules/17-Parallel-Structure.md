# 17 — Parallel Structure

## หลักการ Parallel Structure

> คำ วลี หรือประโยคที่ทำหน้าที่เดียวกันในประโยค ต้องมีรูปแบบไวยากรณ์เดียวกัน

**กฎ**: เชื่อมสิ่งที่ "เหมือนกัน" ด้วย conjunction

---

## Parallel Items ใน List

> ทุก item ต้องเป็น grammatical form เดียวกัน

| ผิด | ถูก |
|-----|-----|
| The job requires **analysis**, **writing**, and **to present**. | The job requires **analysis**, **writing**, and **presenting**. |
| She is **intelligent**, **hardworking**, and has **ambition**. | She is **intelligent**, **hardworking**, and **ambitious**. |
| We need to **reduce costs**, **improve quality**, and **the timeline** must be met. | We need to **reduce costs**, **improve quality**, and **meet the timeline**. |

---

## Parallel กับ Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

> and / but / or / nor → ทั้งสองฝั่งต้องขนาน

| ผิด | ถูก |
|-----|-----|
| He enjoys **to manage** projects and **leading** teams. | He enjoys **managing** projects and **leading** teams. |
| The report is **concise** but **has many details**. | The report is **concise** but **detailed**. |
| We can **meet in person** or **a video call can be arranged**. | We can **meet in person** or **arrange a video call**. |

---

## Parallel กับ Correlative Conjunctions

> both...and / either...or / neither...nor / not only...but also

| คู่ | ต้องขนาน | ตัวอย่าง |
|----|---------|---------|
| **both...and** | same form | She is **both competent** and **dedicated**. |
| **either...or** | same form | You can **either email** or **call** the support team. |
| **neither...nor** | same form | The plan is **neither feasible** nor **cost-effective**. |
| **not only...but also** | same form | She **not only manages** the team **but also trains** new staff. |

### ผิดที่พบบ่อย
> ❌ She is both **a leader** and **has creativity**.
> ✓ She is both **a leader** and **a creative thinker**.

> ❌ Not only **did he improve** efficiency but also **the morale was raised**.
> ✓ Not only **did he improve** efficiency but also **raised** morale.

---

## Parallel ใน Comparisons

> as...as / -er...than / the more...the more

| ผิด | ถูก |
|-----|-----|
| Working remotely is as **efficient** as **to work** in the office. | Working remotely is as **efficient** as **working** in the office. |
| It is better **to act** than **waiting**. | It is better **to act** than **to wait**. |

## 10 TOEIC Part 5 Parallel Structure Patterns ที่ออกบ่อย

### Pattern 1: Gerund list
> The position involves **scheduling**, **coordinating**, and **supervising** staff.

### Pattern 2: Infinitive list
> The manager agreed **to review**, **to approve**, and **to submit** the final report.

### Pattern 3: Adjective list
> The candidate is **experienced**, **reliable**, and **results-oriented**.

### Pattern 4: Noun phrase list
> The package includes **a welcome letter**, **a user guide**, and **a warranty card**.

### Pattern 5: Verb phrase with and
> The team worked efficiently and **delivered** the project ahead of schedule.

### Pattern 6: Not only...but also
> The new system **not only reduces** costs **but also improves** accuracy.

### Pattern 7: Either...or
> Please submit your application **either online** or **by mail**.

### Pattern 8: Both...and
> The position requires **both written** and **verbal** communication skills.

### Pattern 9: Comparison parallel
> Attending the workshop is more **beneficial** than **reading** the manual alone.

### Pattern 10: Series with semicolons
> The agenda covers three topics: **reviewing** last quarter's results; **discussing** the new strategy; and **assigning** responsibilities.

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| She likes **hiking**, **swim**, and **to cycle**. | She likes **hiking**, **swimming**, and **cycling**. | ต้องเป็น gerund ทั้งหมด |
| The plan is **simple** and **easy to be understood**. | The plan is **simple** and **easy to understand**. | adjective + to-infinitive คู่กัน |
| He was promoted **quickly** and with **recognition**. | He was promoted **quickly** and **prominently**. | adverb + adverb |
| The report must be **accurate**, **complete**, and **it must be on time**. | The report must be **accurate**, **complete**, and **timely**. | adjective series ต้องขนาน |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (8 ข้อ)

1. The new software helps employees track expenses, manage budgets, and _______ reports.
   **(A) generation of (B) generating (C) to generate (D) generate**
   → **D** (parallel กับ track, manage = base verb form หลัง helps)

2. Ms. Rivera is known for her attention to detail, her professionalism, and _______.
   **(A) she is dedicated (B) her dedication (C) dedicating (D) dedicated**
   → **B** (parallel กับ noun phrases: her attention, her professionalism → her dedication)

3. The training program is designed not only to improve skills but also _______ confidence.
   **(A) building (B) builds (C) to build (D) built**
   → **C** (not only to improve... but also to build = parallel infinitives)

4. Either the regional manager or the assistant director _______ the conference.
   **(A) are attending (B) is attending (C) attend (D) have attended**
   → **B** (either...or → verb follows nearest subject = director = singular)

5. The merger will allow both companies _______ resources and expand their market reach.
   **(A) sharing (B) to share (C) share (D) shared**
   → **B** (allow + object + to-infinitive; parallel: to share...and expand)

6. Our priorities are _______ customer satisfaction, reducing overhead, and retaining talent.
   **(A) to maximize (B) maximizing (C) maximize (D) maximized**
   → **B** (parallel กับ gerunds: reducing, retaining)

7. The proposal was praised for being thorough, well-researched, and _______.
   **(A) persuasively (B) persuasion (C) persuasive (D) persuaded**
   → **C** (parallel กับ adjectives: thorough, well-researched)

8. The CEO announced plans to restructure the company, _______ costs, and hire new talent.
   **(A) reduce (B) reducing (C) reduced (D) reduction in**
   → **A** (parallel กับ to restructure..., reduce..., hire = infinitives ต่อจาก to)
