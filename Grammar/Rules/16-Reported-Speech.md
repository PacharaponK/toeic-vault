# 16 — Reported Speech

## Tense Backshift Rules

> เมื่อ reporting verb อยู่ใน **past tense** → ถอยเวลาใน reported clause

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|--------------|----------------|
| Simple Present | Simple Past |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect |
| Simple Past | Past Perfect |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | must / had to |
| shall | should |

### ตัวอย่าง
> Direct: "The report **is** ready."
> Reported: He said (that) the report **was** ready.

> Direct: "We **have completed** the audit."
> Reported: They announced (that) they **had completed** the audit.

> Direct: "I **will** send the invoice."
> Reported: She promised (that) she **would** send the invoice.

## Pronoun Changes

> ปรับ pronoun ให้ตรงกับบริบทของผู้รายงาน

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|---------|
| I / me / my / mine | he/she / him/her / his/her / his/hers |
| we / us / our | they / them / their |
| you | I/we (ขึ้นอยู่กับบริบท) |
| my / our | his/her / their |

## Time and Place Changes

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|---------|
| now | then / at that time |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week | the previous week / the week before |
| next month | the following month |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |

## Reporting Verbs ที่หลากหลาย

### + that-clause
| Verb | ความหมาย | ตัวอย่าง |
|------|----------|---------|
| **say** | พูด | He said **that** the budget was approved. |
| **announce** | ประกาศ | They announced **that** the merger was complete. |
| **explain** | อธิบาย | She explained **that** the delay was unavoidable. |
| **admit** | ยอมรับ | He admitted **that** he had made an error. |
| **claim** | อ้าง | She claimed **that** the data was accurate. |
| **confirm** | ยืนยัน | He confirmed **that** the meeting was on. |

### + object + that-clause
| Verb | ตัวอย่าง |
|------|---------|
| **tell** | She told **us** that the project was delayed. |
| **inform** | He informed **the board** that profits had risen. |
| **remind** | She reminded **him** that the deadline was Friday. |
| **warn** | He warned **us** that costs might increase. |
| **convince** | She convinced **them** that the plan was viable. |
| **assure** | He assured **the client** that delivery was on track. |

### + infinitive (request/advice)
| Verb | ตัวอย่าง |
|------|---------|
| **ask** | She asked **him to** submit by noon. |
| **tell** | He told **us to** attend the briefing. |
| **advise** | She advised **them to** review the contract. |
| **warn** | He warned **her not to** sign without counsel. |
| **encourage** | She encouraged **him to** apply. |
| **urge** | They urged **the team to** meet the deadline. |

## กรณีที่ไม่ต้อง Backshift (No Backshift)

> ในบางกรณี tense ในประโยค reported **ไม่ต้องเปลี่ยน** แม้ reporting verb จะเป็น past

### 1. Universal Truths / Scientific Facts
> She **said** (that) the Earth **orbits** the sun. (ความจริงสากล → ไม่เปลี่ยน)
> He **explained** (that) interest rates **affect** consumer spending.

### 2. ข้อเท็จจริงที่ยังเป็นจริงอยู่ในปัจจุบัน
> The CEO **announced** (that) the headquarters **is** in Singapore. (ยังอยู่อยู่)
> She **confirmed** (that) the deadline **is** next Friday. (ยังเป็นอยู่)

### 3. คำพูดที่เพิ่งพูด (just said)
> He just **told** me the meeting **starts** at 3 p.m.

### 4. Backshift Optional เมื่อ reporting verb เป็น present tense
> She **says** (that) the report **is** ready. (present → no change needed)
> He **tells** us (that) profits **have** increased.

## Reported Questions — Word Order Changes

### Yes/No Questions → if / whether
> Direct: "**Have** you reviewed the contract?"
> Reported: She asked **whether / if** he **had** reviewed the contract.

### Wh- Questions → wh-word + declarative order
> Direct: "**What** does the policy **say**?"
> Reported: He asked **what** the policy **said**.

> Direct: "**When** will the results **be** announced?"
> Reported: She asked **when** the results **would be** announced.

---

## Reported Commands and Requests

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|---------|
| "Submit the report." (command) | He told her **to submit** the report. |
| "Please send the file." (request) | She asked him **to send** the file. |
| "Don't be late." (negative command) | He warned them **not to be** late. |
| "Could you review this?" (request) | She asked him **to review** it. |

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| She said me that... | She told me that... / She said that... | say ไม่ตามด้วย object โดยตรง |
| He asked where **was** the office. | He asked where the office **was**. | reported question = declarative word order |
| They told to submit by noon. | They told **us** to submit by noon. | tell ต้องมี object |
| She explained **me** how it worked. | She explained **to me** how it worked. | explain ไม่ตามด้วย indirect object โดยตรง |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (6 ข้อ)

1. The supervisor _______ the team that the new policy would take effect immediately.
   **(A) said (B) told (C) spoke (D) mentioned**
   → **B** (told + object + that-clause)

2. The client asked the consultant _______ an alternative approach.
   **(A) suggest (B) suggested (C) to suggest (D) that suggest**
   → **C** (ask + object + to-infinitive)

3. The manager explained _______ the delay had occurred due to shipping issues.
   **(A) that (B) what (C) how (D) whether**
   → **A** (explain + that-clause)

4. The HR director announced that the salary review _______ place the following month.
   **(A) will take (B) takes (C) would take (D) had taken**
   → **C** (announced = past → will → would; the following month replaces next month)

5. She asked her assistant _______ the conference room for the Thursday meeting.
   **(A) to book (B) booking (C) that book (D) books**
   → **A** (ask + object + to-infinitive)

6. The CFO confirmed _______ the annual profit had exceeded expectations.
   **(A) what (B) that (C) how (D) when**
   → **B** (confirm + that-clause)

7. The scientist explained that water _______ at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level.
   **(A) boiled (B) boils (C) had boiled (D) was boiling**
   → **B** (universal truth → no backshift; present tense คงไว้)

8. The project lead asked the team _______ the prototype by the end of the week.
   **(A) finish (B) finishing (C) to finish (D) that finish**
   → **C** (ask + object + to-infinitive)
