# 08 — Pronouns

## Subject / Object / Possessive Pronouns

| Subject | Object | Possessive Adj | Possessive Pronoun | Reflexive |
|---------|--------|----------------|-------------------|-----------|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | its | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

> **its** (possessive) ≠ **it's** (it is) — ข้อผิดพลาดที่ออก TOEIC บ่อยมาก

---

## Reflexive Pronouns — กฎการใช้

### ใช้เพื่อ Emphasis (เน้น)
> The director **himself** reviewed the contract.
> We **ourselves** are responsible for the outcome.

### ใช้เป็น Object (ผู้กระทำ = ผู้รับ)
> She introduced **herself** to the new team.
> Please help **yourself** to the refreshments.
> The machine turned **itself** off automatically.

### สำนวน by + reflexive = ทำคนเดียว / โดยลำพัง
> He completed the report by **himself**.
> The system rebooted by **itself**.

---

## Relative Pronouns — กฎการเลือก

| Pronoun | ใช้กับ | บทบาทใน clause |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| **who** | คน (subject) | The manager **who** signed the contract... |
| **whom** | คน (object) | The candidate **whom** we interviewed... |
| **which** | สิ่ง/สัตว์ | The report, **which** was submitted late... |
| **that** | คน/สิ่ง (defining only) | The document **that** you requested... |
| **whose** | แสดงความเป็นเจ้าของ | The employee **whose** proposal was accepted... |

### who vs whom
- **who** = subject → The manager **who** leads the team
- **whom** = object → The manager **whom** I met / to **whom** I spoke
- เคล็ดลับ: แทนด้วย he/him → he = who / him = whom

### which vs that
- **that** → defining clause เท่านั้น (ไม่มี comma)
- **which** → non-defining clause (มี comma) หรือ defining ก็ได้
- หลัง preposition ใช้ **which** เสมอ → the project **in which** we invested

---

## Indefinite Pronouns

| คำ | กริยา | ตัวอย่าง |
|----|-------|---------|
| **each / every** | singular | **Each** employee **has** a login. |
| **either / neither** | singular | **Neither** option **is** acceptable. |
| **everyone / someone / anyone** | singular | **Everyone** **is** welcome. |
| **both / several / few / many** | plural | **Both** candidates **are** qualified. |
| **all / some / none** | ดูที่ noun หลัง of | **All of the information is** accurate. / **All of the reports are** ready. |

---

## Pronoun Agreement Rules

### กฎ 1: ตรงกับ antecedent ในด้าน gender/number
> Each employee must submit **his or her** timesheet.
> Employees must submit **their** timesheets.

### กฎ 2: Collective noun → singular pronoun (ทั่วไปในภาษาอังกฤษแบบ American)
> The committee made **its** decision.
> The team completed **its** project on time.

### กฎ 3: everyone/someone/anyone → traditionally singular
> **Everyone** should bring **their** ID. *(acceptable in modern usage)*
> **No one** has submitted **their** form yet.

## Common Mistakes ใน TOEIC

| ผิด | ถูก | เหตุผล |
|-----|-----|--------|
| The company increased **it's** profit. | The company increased **its** profit. | possessive = its (ไม่มี apostrophe) |
| The director **hisself** approved. | The director **himself** approved. | hisself ไม่มีในภาษาอังกฤษ |
| **Who** did you hire? | **Whom** did you hire? | hire = transitive verb → object = whom |
| The report **which** I mentioned it | The report **which** I mentioned | ไม่ต้องมี pronoun ซ้ำหลัง relative clause |
| Between you and **I** | Between you and **me** | หลัง preposition → object pronoun |
| **Myself** will present. | **I** will present. | reflexive ไม่ใช้เป็น subject ลำพัง |

## ตัวอย่างประโยค TOEIC Part 5 Style (6 ข้อ)

1. The HR department will contact each applicant about _______ interview schedule.
   **(A) their (B) his or her (C) its (D) B is correct, A is also acceptable**
   → **D** (ทั้ง "their" และ "his or her" ใช้ได้; TOEIC มักเลือก B)

2. The CEO _______ approved the budget without consulting the board.
   **(A) him (B) his (C) himself (D) he**
   → **C** (reflexive เพื่อเน้น — the CEO himself)

3. The analyst _______ report was published last week received an award.
   **(A) who (B) which (C) whose (D) whom**
   → **C** (whose + noun = possession)

4. The new regulations apply to all employees, regardless of _______ position.
   **(A) them (B) their (C) they (D) theirs**
   → **B** (possessive adjective before noun)

5. Neither the manager nor the assistants have submitted _______ reports.
   **(A) his (B) its (C) their (D) her**
   → **C** (neither...nor → verb/pronoun follows nearest noun = assistants → their)

6. The committee reached _______ decision after a lengthy debate.
   **(A) their (B) its (C) his (D) our**
   → **B** (committee = collective noun → singular → its)
