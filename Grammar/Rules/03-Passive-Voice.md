# 03 — Passive Voice

## โครงสร้าง Passive ทุก Tense

| Tense | Active | Passive (be + V3) |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| Simple Present | writes | is/are written |
| Simple Past | wrote | was/were written |
| Simple Future | will write | will be written |
| Present Continuous | is writing | is/are being written |
| Past Continuous | was writing | was/were being written |
| Present Perfect | has written | has/have been written |
| Past Perfect | had written | had been written |
| Future Perfect | will have written | will have been written |

---

## Passive + Modal Verbs

| Modal | โครงสร้าง | ตัวอย่าง |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| can | can be + V3 | The form can be downloaded online. |
| must | must be + V3 | All invoices must be approved by the CFO. |
| should | should be + V3 | Errors should be reported immediately. |
| may/might | may/might be + V3 | The meeting may be rescheduled. |
| need to | need to be + V3 | The contract needs to be reviewed. |
| have to | have to be + V3 | The report has to be submitted by noon. |

---

## Stative Passive (ใช้บ่อยใน TOEIC)

Stative passive = passive ที่แสดงสถานะ ไม่ใช่การกระทำ มักใช้กับ be + V3 + preposition

| Phrase | ตัวอย่าง |
|--------|---------|
| be interested in | She is interested in the new position. |
| be located in/at | The office is located in the city center. |
| be involved in | He is involved in the merger negotiation. |
| be concerned about | Management is concerned about the delays. |
| be dedicated to | The team is dedicated to quality service. |
| be known for | The firm is known for its innovative products. |
| be based in | The company is based in Singapore. |
| be satisfied with | Clients are satisfied with the results. |
| be committed to | We are committed to customer satisfaction. |
| be required to | All employees are required to attend. |
| be scheduled to | The launch is scheduled to begin next month. |
| be responsible for | She is responsible for the financial report. |

---

## Active → Passive Transformation Rules

1. Object ของ Active กลายเป็น Subject ของ Passive
2. กริยาเปลี่ยนเป็น be + V3 (be ต้องตรง tense กับ active)
3. Subject ของ Active กลายเป็น by + agent (หรือตัดทิ้งถ้าไม่สำคัญ)

**ตัวอย่าง:**
> Active: The company **launched** the new product last month.
> Passive: The new product **was launched** (by the company) last month.

> Active: They **are reviewing** all applications.
> Passive: All applications **are being reviewed**.

> Active: The team **has completed** the project.
> Passive: The project **has been completed** (by the team).

## TOEIC Part 5 Passive Patterns ที่ออกบ่อย

### Pattern 1: be + V3 (basic passive)
> Applicants **are required** to submit two references.
> All products **are tested** before shipment.

### Pattern 2: Get + V3 (informal passive)
> The report **got lost** in the system. (น้อยกว่า be passive ใน TOEIC)

### Pattern 3: by + agent (ระบุผู้กระทำ)
> The annual budget **was approved** by the board of directors.

### Pattern 4: Passive infinitive
> The documents need **to be signed** before the deadline.
> Staff are expected **to be trained** on the new system.

### Pattern 5: Passive + prepositional phrase (location/result)
> The new branch **will be opened** in downtown Bangkok next year.
> The system **was designed** for maximum efficiency.

## กฎเลือก Active vs Passive ใน TOEIC

| ใช้ Passive เมื่อ | ใช้ Active เมื่อ |
|------------------|-----------------|
| ไม่รู้ว่าใครเป็นผู้กระทำ | รู้ผู้กระทำชัดเจน |
| ผู้กระทำไม่สำคัญ | ต้องการเน้นผู้กระทำ |
| ต้องการเน้น object | ต้องการเน้น subject |
| สไตล์ formal/business writing | ประโยคทั่วไป |

## ตัวอย่าง TOEIC Part 5 Style (5 ข้อ)

1. All expense reports must _______ to the finance department by the last Friday of each month.
   **(A) submit (B) be submitted (C) be submitting (D) have submitted**
   → **B** (must + be + V3 = passive modal)

2. The new headquarters _______ in the northern district of the city last year.
   **(A) is constructing (B) was constructing (C) was constructed (D) has constructed**
   → **C** (last year = simple past passive)

3. The merger agreement _______ by both CEOs before it can take effect.
   **(A) needs signed (B) needs to be signed (C) needs signing (D) is needing to sign**
   → **B** (needs to be + V3)

4. Employees _______ to report any safety hazards to their supervisors immediately.
   **(A) encourage (B) are encouraged (C) encouraging (D) have encouraged**
   → **B** (stative passive: are encouraged to)

5. The financial audit _______ when the new compliance officer joined the team.
   **(A) was being conducted (B) is being conducted (C) has been conducted (D) will be conducted**
   → **A** (ongoing past action interrupted → past continuous passive)
