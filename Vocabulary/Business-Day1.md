# Business English — Week 1

---

## 1. allocate #flashcard

**ความหมาย:** จัดสรร, แบ่งปัน (ทรัพยากร เงิน เวลา)

**ตัวอย่าง TOEIC-style:**
1. The finance department will **allocate** a larger budget to the marketing division next quarter.
2. Management has decided to **allocate** additional staff to the customer service team during peak season.

**Synonyms:** assign, distribute, apportion

**TOEIC Frequency:** High

---

## 2. comply #flashcard

**ความหมาย:** ปฏิบัติตาม, ทำตามกฎหรือคำสั่ง

**ตัวอย่าง TOEIC-style:**
1. All employees are required to **comply** with the new workplace safety regulations by the end of the month.
2. The supplier failed to **comply** with the terms outlined in the contract, resulting in a penalty.

**Synonyms:** adhere (to), conform (to), abide (by)

**TOEIC Frequency:** High

---

## 3. negotiate #flashcard

**ความหมาย:** เจรจา, ต่อรอง

**ตัวอย่าง TOEIC-style:**
1. The two companies spent three weeks trying to **negotiate** a mutually beneficial licensing agreement.
2. Ms. Park was sent to **negotiate** better shipping rates with the overseas logistics provider.

**Synonyms:** bargain, discuss, mediate

**TOEIC Frequency:** High

---

## 4. scrutinize #flashcard

**ความหมาย:** ตรวจสอบอย่างละเอียด, พิจารณาอย่างถี่ถ้วน

**ตัวอย่าง TOEIC-style:**
1. The auditors were asked to **scrutinize** every financial transaction from the previous fiscal year.
2. Before signing the partnership deal, the legal team carefully **scrutinized** each clause of the agreement.

**Synonyms:** examine, inspect, analyze

**TOEIC Frequency:** Medium

---

## 5. delegate #flashcard

**ความหมาย:** มอบหมายงาน, มอบอำนาจให้ผู้อื่น

**ตัวอย่าง TOEIC-style:**
1. Effective managers know how to **delegate** responsibilities so that no single team member is overburdened.
2. The CEO **delegated** the authority to approve travel expenses to each department head.

**Synonyms:** assign, entrust, transfer

**TOEIC Frequency:** Medium
