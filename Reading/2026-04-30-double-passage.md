# TOEIC Part 7 — Double Passage
**Topic:** Office Renovation and Temporary Relocation

---

## Document 1 — Email

**From:** Thomas Ward, Operations Manager
**To:** All Staff
**Date:** April 30, 2026
**Subject:** Temporary Relocation — Main Office Renovation

Dear Team,

I am writing to inform you that our main office at 15 Meridian Tower will undergo a scheduled **renovation** beginning May 15. The renovation is expected to last approximately six weeks.

During this period, all departments will temporarily **relocate** to our annex building at 22 Harbor Street, Suite 400. The facilities team will transfer all office equipment and furniture between May 12 and May 14.

To ensure a smooth transition, all employees are asked to pack their personal belongings and label any desk items by end of day on May 11. Access cards for the annex building will be distributed by your department heads on May 13.

We understand that this may cause some inconvenience, and we appreciate your patience and cooperation during this time. Please do not hesitate to contact me directly if you have any questions regarding the move.

Best regards,
Thomas Ward
Operations Manager

---

## Document 2 — Memo

**MEMORANDUM**

**To:** All Employees
**From:** Human Resources Department
**Date:** April 30, 2026
**Re:** Relocation Schedule and Remote Work Policy

Following the announcement from the Operations Manager regarding the upcoming office relocation, HR would like to provide the following guidelines.

Employees who wish to work remotely during the six-week renovation period may do so for up to two days per week. To be **eligible**, employees must submit a completed Remote Work Request Form to HR no later than May 9. Forms are available on the company intranet.

A detailed daily schedule for the relocation process will be posted on the notice board by May 5. For any inquiries, please contact Sandra Lee, HR Coordinator, at extension 204.

We thank all employees in advance for their understanding and cooperation.

Human Resources Department

## เฉลยและ Line Reference

> [!warning] หยุดก่อน! ตอบทุกข้อแล้วค่อยเปิดดู

**Q1 → B**
Doc 1, ย่อหน้า 1: *"our main office … will undergo a scheduled renovation … all departments will temporarily relocate"*
จุดประสงค์หลักของ email คือแจ้งการย้ายสำนักงานชั่วคราวเนื่องจากการปรับปรุง

**Q2 → C (May 11)**
Doc 1, ย่อหน้า 3: *"pack their personal belongings and label any desk items by end of day on May 11"*
วันที่ 11 พ.ค. คือ deadline ในการแพ็คของ — อย่าสับสนกับวันที่ย้ายอุปกรณ์ (12–14) หรือวันรับบัตร (13)

**Q3 → D (Two days)**
Doc 2, ย่อหน้า 2: *"may do so for up to two days per week"*

**Q4 → C**
Doc 2, ย่อหน้า 2: *"employees must submit a completed Remote Work Request Form to HR no later than May 9"*
ต้องส่งฟอร์มภายใน 9 พ.ค. — ไม่ใช่ขอจากหัวหน้า และไม่ต้องโทรหา Sandra Lee

**Q5 → B**
- Doc 1, ย่อหน้า 1: *"expected to last approximately **six weeks**"*
- Doc 2, ย่อหน้า 2: *"during the **six-week** renovation period"*
ทั้ง 2 docs กล่าวถึงระยะเวลาปรับปรุง 6 สัปดาห์ตรงกัน — ตัวเลือกอื่นมาจาก doc เดียวเท่านั้น
