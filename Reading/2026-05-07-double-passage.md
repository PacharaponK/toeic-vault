# TOEIC Part 7 — Double Passage Practice

**Topic:** Mandatory Training for New Procurement Management System
**Documents:** Email + Internal Memo

---

## 📄 Document 1 — Email (~150 words)

---

**From:** Helen Royce, Chief Procurement Officer
**To:** All Procurement Department Staff
**Date:** May 7, 2026
**Subject:** Mandatory Training — New Procurement Management System

Dear Team,

I am pleased to announce that our company will be transitioning to a new Procurement Management System (PMS) on August 1. To ensure all staff are fully prepared, the department will undergo mandatory two-day training at the end of June.

Training has been divided into two groups to minimize operational disruption. Group A will attend on **June 16**, and Group B on **June 17**. Sessions run from 9:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M. in **Conference Suite 6A**. Assigned groups will be communicated via email by **May 20**. Laptops will be pre-configured by IT and distributed at the start of each session.

Staff who are unable to attend their assigned date must email training@company.com by **June 1** to request an alternative arrangement. Please note that rescheduling cannot be guaranteed.

Further details and a pre-reading manual will be available on the company intranet from May 20.

Regards,
Helen Royce
Chief Procurement Officer

---

## 📄 Document 2 — Internal Memo (~120 words)

---

**From:** Marcus Webb, Purchasing Manager
**To:** Purchasing Team
**Date:** May 7, 2026
**Re:** PMS Training — Purchasing Team Schedule and Preparation

Following Ms. Royce's announcement, I am writing to confirm that our team has been assigned to **Group A** and will therefore attend training on **June 16**.

To prepare effectively, please ensure that all outstanding purchase orders are processed and submitted by **June 13**. You are also encouraged to review the pre-reading materials once they become available on the intranet on May 20.

During our team's absence on June 16, the **Supplier Relations team** will manage any urgent procurement requests on our behalf.

Following the training, both the current and new systems will run **simultaneously** for a transition period of two to three weeks. I will hold a team briefing in the week of **June 23** to address any remaining questions.

Marcus Webb
Purchasing Manager

## ✅ เฉลยและคำอธิบาย

---

**ข้อ 1 — คำตอบ: (B)** ✅
*To inform procurement staff about mandatory training for a new software system*

> **Line reference (Doc 1, ย่อหน้า 1–2):**
> *"…our company will be transitioning to a new **Procurement Management System**… the department will undergo **mandatory** two-day training…"*

อีเมลมีวัตถุประสงค์หลักคือแจ้งข่าวการ training ที่บังคับ ไม่ใช่ขอ feedback (C) หรือนัดประชุม (D)
(A) ผิดเพราะ current system จะหยุดใช้ August 1 ไม่ใช่ June 16

---

**ข้อ 2 — คำตอบ: (C)** ✅
*Notification of which training group they have been assigned to*

> **Line reference (Doc 1, ย่อหน้า 2):**
> *"Assigned groups will be **communicated via email by May 20**."*

(A) ผิดเพราะ laptops จะ distribute ที่ session ไม่ใช่ภายใน May 20
(B) ผิดเพราะ deadline ขอเปลี่ยน group คือ June 1 (ไม่ใช่ May 20)
(D) ผิดเพราะ pre-reading manual เป็นสิ่งที่จะได้รับ May 20 ไม่ใช่ system access

---

**ข้อ 3 — คำตอบ: (C)** ✅
*All outstanding purchase orders*

> **Line reference (Doc 2, ย่อหน้า 2):**
> *"…please ensure that all **outstanding purchase orders** are processed and submitted by **June 13**."*

June 13 เป็น internal deadline ของ Purchasing team ก่อน training วันที่ June 16
(A)(B)(D) ไม่มีการกล่าวถึงใน documents

---

**ข้อ 4 — คำตอบ: (B)** ✅
*The Supplier Relations team will manage them temporarily*

> **Line reference (Doc 2, ย่อหน้า 3):**
> *"…the **Supplier Relations team** will manage any urgent procurement requests on our behalf."*

(A)(C)(D) ไม่ตรงกับข้อมูลในเอกสาร — Mr. Webb ระบุชัดว่า Supplier Relations รับผิดชอบแทน

---

**ข้อ 5 — คำตอบ: (C)** ✅
*They will have already completed their mandatory PMS training.*

> **Line reference รวม 2 docs:**
> - Doc 1, ย่อหน้า 2: *"Group A will attend on **June 16**, and Group B on **June 17**."*
> - Doc 2, ย่อหน้า 1: *"our team has been assigned to **Group A** and will therefore attend training on **June 16**."*

ต้องอ่านข้อมูลจากทั้ง 2 docs — Doc 1 ระบุว่า Group A = June 16, Doc 2 ยืนยันว่า Purchasing = Group A → ดังนั้นในวันที่ June 17 Purchasing team ผ่าน training ไปแล้ว

(A) ผิดเพราะ Group B เท่านั้นที่ attend June 17 — Purchasing เป็น Group A
(B) ผิดเพราะ Supplier Relations ดูแลแทน Purchasing ใน June 16 ไม่ใช่กลับกัน
(D) ไม่มีข้อมูลใน document ยืนยัน
